## What it detects

`waitgroupsafety/negative-counter` is experimental. It reports a `Done` that
decrements a known zero count, including completion hidden in helpers or defers.
Local and cross-package helpers use the shared compositional concurrency summaries.

The proof requires a fresh local WaitGroup and complete ordered effects. It models
`Add(1)`, `Done`, and `Wait`. A single worker is supported when its only effects
are `Done` calls and the caller only waits after launching it. This establishes
the worker's starting count without exploring schedules.

Borrowed groups, other Add amounts, opaque calls, loops, divergent effects,
additional workers, and resets remain unknown. A prior blocking wait ends the
proof. This is not a general missing-Done, group-reuse, or WaitGroup.Go check, and
does not duplicate vet's Add-inside-goroutine check.
