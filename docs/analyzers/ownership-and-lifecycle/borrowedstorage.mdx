## What it detects

Reports borrowed mutable storage transferred to another escaping owner without
first making a copy.

### Checks

{/* gohawk:generated-checks:start */}
| Check | Kind | Tier | What it detects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <CheckIdentity name="overlapping-owner" tier="experimental" /> | hazard | experimental | Reports borrowed bytes.Buffer storage transferred to a second escaping owner without a copy. |
{/* gohawk:generated-checks:end */}

## Why this is flagged

Two owners can independently mutate or recycle one backing array. For example,
`bytes.NewBuffer` takes ownership of its input, while `Buffer.Bytes` returns a
view that remains owned by the source buffer.

## How to fix it

Clone the borrowed bytes before passing them to an ownership-taking API. A
local handoff is also accepted when the source owner cannot outlive the call.
