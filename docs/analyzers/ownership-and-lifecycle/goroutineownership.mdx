## What it detects

Reports goroutines whose join handle or lifecycle owner is not honored on every return path.

### Checks

{/* gohawk:generated-checks:start */}
| Check | Kind | What it detects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <CheckIdentity name="unjoined" /> | hazard | Reports goroutines with a recognizable join or lifecycle mechanism that is not honored on every return path. |
| <CheckIdentity name="detached" optIn /> | hazard | Reports goroutines without a recognizable join handle or lifecycle owner. |

\* Opt-in; requires explicit selection.
{/* gohawk:generated-checks:end */}

## Why this is flagged

Unowned goroutines can outlive their inputs, retain resources, and make shutdown nondeterministic.

## How to fix it

Join the goroutine, transfer its lifecycle explicitly, or connect it to a bounded owner.
