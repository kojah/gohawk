## What it detects

Reports large constant channel capacities without a nearby bounded rationale.

### Checks

{/* gohawk:generated-checks:start */}
| Check | Kind | Tier | What it detects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <CheckIdentity name="rationale" tier="extended" /> | policy | extended | Reports large constant channel capacities in production files without a nearby bounded rationale. |
{/* gohawk:generated-checks:end */}

## Why this is flagged

A channel's buffer size is a design decision: it sets how many sends can outrun
their receiver before the sender blocks. A large constant with no explanation
hides that backpressure and quietly bakes in an operational bound nobody wrote
down, so a later reader cannot tell whether the number is deliberate or
arbitrary.

Further reading: [Effective Go: Channels](https://go.dev/doc/effective_go#channels).

## How to fix it

Use a smaller channel or document what bounds the queue.
