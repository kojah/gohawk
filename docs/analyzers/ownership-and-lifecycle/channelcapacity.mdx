## What it detects

Reports large constant channel capacities without a nearby bounded rationale.

### Checks

{/* gohawk:generated-checks:start */}
| Check | What it detects |
| --- | --- |
| <CheckIdentity name="rationale" optIn /> | Reports large constant channel capacities in production files without a nearby bounded rationale. |

\* Opt-in; requires explicit selection.
{/* gohawk:generated-checks:end */}

## Why this is flagged

An unexplained buffer can hide backpressure and encode an undocumented operational bound.

## How to fix it

Use a smaller channel or document what bounds the queue.
