## What it detects

Reports functions that close channels borrowed from their callers.

### Checks

{/* gohawk:generated-checks:start */}
| Check | What it detects |
| --- | --- |
| <CheckIdentity name="caller-close" /> | Reports functions that close channels received from their callers. |
{/* gohawk:generated-checks:end */}

## Why this is flagged

Closing from the wrong layer can race with the sender and panic.

## How to fix it

Let the channel creator or designated producer close the channel after its final send.
