## What it detects

Reports a callee close only when an exact caller continues to send, close, or return that same channel.

### Checks

| <CheckIdentity name="caller-close" tier="extended" /> | policy | extended | Reports callees that close a channel an exact caller continues to use. |
{/* gohawk:generated-checks:end */}

## Why this is flagged

Only the code that owns a channel should close it. When a callee closes a
channel that its caller still sends on, closes, or returns, the close can race
with the caller's send — and a send on a closed channel panics. By convention
the sender decides when a channel is done, not a helper it calls.

Further reading: [Go concurrency patterns: Pipelines and cancellation](https://go.dev/blog/pipelines).

## How to fix it

Choose one close owner and make the caller stop using the channel after handing it off.
