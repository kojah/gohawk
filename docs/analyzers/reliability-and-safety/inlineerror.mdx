## What it detects

Reports inline error declarations whose condition accidentally checks a different error.

### Checks

{/* gohawk:generated-checks:start */}
| Check | Kind | What it detects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <CheckIdentity name="mismatched-condition" /> | defect | Reports inline error declarations whose condition checks a different error. |
{/* gohawk:generated-checks:end */}

## Why this is flagged

Checking an older error can silently ignore the failure that was just returned.

## How to fix it

Reference the newly declared error in the `if` condition.
