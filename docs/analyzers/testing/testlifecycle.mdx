## What it detects

Reports detached test goroutines rooted in a never-cancelled context when they observe cancellation.

### Checks

{/* gohawk:generated-checks:start */}
| Check | What it detects |
| --- | --- |
| <CheckIdentity name="context-root" optIn /> | Reports detached test-owned goroutines rooted in a never-cancelled context. |

\* Opt-in; requires explicit selection.
{/* gohawk:generated-checks:end */}

## Why this is flagged

Detached work can survive test teardown and interfere with later tests.

## How to fix it

Use `t.Context()` or `b.Context()`, or explicitly cancel and join the worker.
