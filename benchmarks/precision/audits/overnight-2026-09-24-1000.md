# September 24 frozen 1,000-repository audit

Four fresh, deduplicated sets of 250 pinned GitHub Go repositories were
scanned with the same `7305cc9` gohawk binary (SHA-256
`576caba104c1f436c3791268e2c6cea40e3585315a34fa84363f458a947ba5ec`),
`-enable-all -gohawk-include-tests -json`, and static analysis only. No
candidate tests, generators, or applications were executed. The [batch 56](batch-56.md),
[batch 57](batch-57.md), [batch 58](batch-58.md), and [batch 59](batch-59.md)
records link their pin and selection receipts, scan errors, and exact
per-finding source judgments. Scanner-runner changes during batches 56–58
are versioned in scan receipts; they did not change the frozen analyzer.

The 1,883 emitted findings all have a source-backed verdict. Complete
scans with no finding are not a recall estimate, and the 231 incomplete
or failed repositories must not be treated as clean. A true-positive
verdict means the reported source path violates the check's contract; it
does not mean the candidate project reproduced a runtime failure or
accepted a patch. Later analyzer fixes retain separate replay receipts.

These totals are frozen-baseline, finding-weighted counts, not independent
bug-family counts. Test-heavy repositories contribute many repeated
resource sites; for example, batch 59's Twitch test suite alone contributed
257 findings. Experimental `nilargument` produced only two true positives
against 297 false positives across all four batches. It should remain
experimental; retirement or a much narrower proof should be assessed
against corrected pinned replays and true-positive controls, not by
broadening possible-nil claims.

## Explicit follow-ups, separate from the audit result

1. Automatically remove only task-owned checkouts after their batch is
   fully source-reviewed and sealed, validating pins and exact targets;
   retain manifests, scan reports, and finding ledgers for regeneration.
2. Tighten `resourcelifetime` around proven bodyless HTTP responses
   (HTTP 204 and exact zero-length responses) and ownership transferred
   into returned builders or framework stream handlers. Preserve
   nonempty-response and pre-transfer error-path true-positive controls.
3. Investigate low-hit concurrency recall by counting candidate rejection
   reasons before expanding proofs. Candidate models include exact channel
   identity, ordered helper effects, bounded `select` alternatives, and
   exact child-goroutine multiplicity; they must remain bounded and
   inconclusive when identities or participants are unknown.
4. Relate synchronization-graph context-cancellation identity to `Done`
   readiness without treating cancellation itself as a join or a proof
   that a worker terminated.
5. Evaluate source-backed precision gaps such as conditional deferred
   lock release through helpers, alongside the other individually
   reviewed false-positive families. Preserve the original finding labels
   and verify each correction with accepted and diagnostic controls.

This is the current follow-up list for this audit, not a claim that every
historical idea has been filed or implemented.
