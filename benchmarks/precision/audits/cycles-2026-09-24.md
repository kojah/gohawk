# Cycle-check recall audit (2026-09-24)

This targeted follow-up asks whether the four experimental dependency-cycle
checks now report anything on ordinary repositories, and records why they stay
silent when they do. The checks are `lockorder/lock-and-join`,
`lockorder/channel-lock-cycle`, `lockorder/waitgroup-lock-cycle`, and
`channelsafety/dependency-cycle`. In the frozen
[1,000-repository audit](overnight-2026-09-24-1000.md) they emitted no
findings.

## Selection and scan

The [ledger](cycles-2026-09-24.tsv) lists 100 repositories drawn with a fixed
seed from the 769 complete scans in batches 56–59, limited to the 110 whose
frozen audit reported any concurrency finding. That keeps repositories with no
goroutines or locks, where silence says nothing, out of the sample. It does
not select for the shape these checks look for. Revisions and modules are the
frozen pins.

The binary was built at `04036b9` (SHA-256
`a6c383e25aeb6afc278f90824d74ba8bb2fa9a5f1083c79de5972a8abf44fef4`), after the
inert-data and easy-tier summary changes recorded in
[the synchronization graph notes](../../../docs/development/synchronization-graph.md).
Each module was analyzed with the standalone CLI, static analysis only:

```sh
CGO_ENABLED=0 GOFLAGS=-mod=readonly GOTOOLCHAIN=local GOWORK=off GOMAXPROCS=2 \
  gohawk -json -gohawk-include-tests \
  -enable-checks=lockorder/lock-and-join,lockorder/channel-lock-cycle,lockorder/waitgroup-lock-cycle,channelsafety/dependency-cycle \
  -gohawk-trace=lockorder,channelsafety -gohawk-trace-file=TRACE ./...
```

98 repositories scanned completely. Two had one incomplete submodule each
(`cadence-go-client/internal/tools` has no packages;
`protolock/plugin-samples/plugin-sample-wasm` needs a `go.mod` update that
`-mod=readonly` refuses); their root modules scanned.

## Result

**No findings.** Counts below come from the evidence traces, limited to each
repository's own source (no `vendor/`, standard library, or module cache) and
deduplicated by check, candidate, reason, and outcome.

95 repositories produced at least one candidate; five produced none. In 67 of
them, at least one candidate passed the cheap source filter and reached
summary construction.

| Check | Stopped by source filter | Summary incomplete | Cycle decision reached | Reported |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `channelsafety/dependency-cycle` | 3,175 | 345 | 0 | 0 |
| `lockorder/lock-and-join` | – | 287 | 13 (rejected) | 0 |
| `lockorder/channel-lock-cycle` | – | 287 | 13 (rejected) | 0 |
| `lockorder/waitgroup-lock-cycle` | – | 61 | 0 | 0 |

The two lock/signal checks share one candidate filter and one summary, so their
rows describe the same sites. The channel filter stops candidates with fewer
than two local channels (2,577) or no visible launch (598).

All 13 cycle decisions are correct rejections: the function creates a channel
and registers it under its lock without ever waiting while holding it. For
example,
[rabbitmq/amqp091-go `deferredConfirmations.Add`](https://github.com/rabbitmq/amqp091-go/blob/a0195c6baf35db642d13651cb28938f899062e7c/confirms.go#L151)
and
[nanomsg/mangos `socket.OpenContext`](https://github.com/nanomsg/mangos/blob/c144032cd9c5d316a53124ee6e3aff70c60aea18/protocol/rep/rep.go#L413)
have this shape.

## Why candidates were missed

The first instruction that stopped each of the 287 incomplete summaries, for
lock-and-join (the same sites as channel-lock-cycle):

| First blocker | In the candidate | In a callee | Test / production |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Loop or unsupported branch | 76 | – | 25 / 51 |
| Call with no usable summary | 70 | 21 | 26 / 65 |
| Field address on an object read from storage | 36 | 2 | 2 / 36 |
| Store that publishes a reference | 25 | – | 4 / 21 |
| Comparison of non-inert values | 12 | – | production |
| Other (phis, lookups, selects, callee channels, …) | 31 | 14 | 9 / 36 |

For `channelsafety/dependency-cycle`, most incomplete candidates are tests:
opaque calls (104 in the test itself, 30 in a callee), loops (64), and 37
channels created inside a test helper rather than the test.

### The testing package is the largest group of opaque calls in tests

Deadlock-shaped code with local locks and channels lives mostly in tests, and
the largest named group of opaque calls is the `testing` package:
`t.Parallel()` (45 candidates), `t.Helper()` (19), `t.Cleanup` (7), and
`t.TempDir()` (5). These calls have no complete summary, so a test that starts
with `t.Parallel()` is unknown before its first channel operation. Example:
[caidaoli/ccLoad `TestRequestCodexUsageSamplesBeforeResetCreditLookupCompletes`](https://github.com/caidaoli/ccLoad/blob/d79f6931111697da13a9f8cb58abb506a62ed81f/internal/app/admin_codex_auth_test.go#L5893).
Their documented contracts do not touch caller channels or locks, but
`Parallel` pauses the test and `Cleanup` registers a callback that runs later,
so modeling them needs a documented standard-library contract at one decision
point, not a name exemption.

### Remaining classes

- **Loops** in production code are mostly real protocol loops, for example
  [arangodb/go-driver `agencyConnection.doOnce`](https://github.com/arangodb/go-driver/blob/8be712a8c5acbb4a7abbf7a935de5aae6650ba01/agency/agency_connection.go#L128)
  (open bead `gohawk-o44.2`).
- **Mutexes on objects read from storage**, for example
  [containerd/nri `stub.Start`](https://github.com/containerd/nri/blob/53b711125c22339d9ba367bce242d9f1dfc6b75a/pkg/stub/stub.go#L376),
  need stable-content evidence for caller storage.
- **Channels created in a helper** such as
  [alpaca-trade-api-go `newMockConn`](https://github.com/alpacahq/alpaca-trade-api-go/blob/5364a1c723677cd8ec5b81beab34e250a6d4f007/marketdata/stream/conn_test.go#L27)
  need constructor results to carry participants (bead `gohawk-o44.4`).
- **Opaque calls** outside `testing` include `time.Now`, `os/signal.Notify`,
  `exec.Command`, `context.WithTimeout`, logging, and interface methods.

Silence here is a measured blind spot, not evidence that the repositories are
free of these deadlocks.
